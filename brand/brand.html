About.

Brand Essence

Since its launch in September 2016, rom&nd has created high-value beauty experiences for Gen Z consumers around the world, and has entered more than 20 countries, including Asia, the Americas, Europe, and the Middle East.

Our Vision

  • Global Service

    The world takes us further. We exist to share better
    beauty experiences with more people, everywhere.

  • No Limit

    We don’t define beauty by
    boundaries— it isn’t limited by country, category, gender, or age.
    We believe beauty has no limits, and we believe you shouldn’t either.

  • Team Works

    We work by communicating with each other. If your team
    member's decision is correct, you can go along with it and
    persuade him if he makes the wrong decision.
